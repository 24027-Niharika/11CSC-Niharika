Endangered Animals in NZ:

Aotearoa’s unique wildlife is in trouble. Over 4,000 of our native species are threatened by introduced pests and losing their homes.

Introduced pests like rats and stoats, along with habitat loss, have made it hard for native animals to survive. From flightless birds like the kākāpō to rare marine mammals like the Māui dolphin, New Zealand’s wildlife is under constant threat from predators and human activity.
